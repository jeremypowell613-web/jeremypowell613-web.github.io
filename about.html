About this checklist

This checklist was put together by Jeremy Powell. I spent 15 years in the motor trade, seven of them as an MOT tester, before moving into building websites for garages and trades through Powell Hosts.

The checklist grew out of a study of 224 UK independent garage, MOT centre and vehicle business websites. Reading through them, the same handful of small, fixable problems kept turning up: phone numbers that did not work on a tap, missing HTTPS, old copyright years, and so on. This page turns those findings into a plain checklist any garage owner can run themselves, in about 10 minutes, with no technical background needed.

Nothing here is our paid method. It is the basic, self-service checks anyone can do on their own site. See the findings page for the published numbers behind it.
