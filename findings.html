Findings behind the checklist

This checklist is based on a study of 224 UK independent garage, MOT centre and vehicle business websites. These are the published aggregate numbers, summarised here. No garage is named.

The sample

224 websites loaded and were checked. A further 14 did not respond and were excluded from the results. Checks were read-only and used a normal desktop Chrome browser identity. Categories below overlap, since a single site can have more than one issue.

32 sites, or 14%, had at least one issue in the study's serious-issue group: no working HTTPS, no viewport setting, mostly broken pictures, a Covid notice, or an apparently unchanged homepage for three years or more. That grouping describes the checks run, not a measured loss of bookings.

Method note

Checks were carried out on 3 November 2026 using read-only, publicly available information: a normal desktop Chrome browser identity visiting each homepage, the plain HTTP address, up to ten images per site, and the Internet Archive's available homepage history. This is a selected sample, not a census or a statistically representative estimate of every UK garage website.

These are the original publisher's aggregate figures, summarised here with no changes to the numbers. For the full write-up, charts and a downloadable summary table, see the source: the Garage Website Study on powellhosts.co.uk.
